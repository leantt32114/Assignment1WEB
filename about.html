RDNA 4 Architecture

RDNA 4 — is AMD's fourth-generation graphics processor architecture, built on a 4nm process with a monolithic (not chiplet) chip design. The RX 9070 XT graphics card, built on this architecture, contains 53.9 billion transistors on a 356.5 mm² die. The architecture features third-generation ray tracing units, third-generation AI accelerators (Matrix Acceleration), and a redesigned cache system. Compared to RDNA 3, these improvements deliver a significant boost in ray tracing performance and a much stronger foundation for AI-based upscaling technologies.

Key Technical Facts

  • 3rd generation ray tracing with doubled ray intersection rates
  • 64 MB of 3rd generation Infinity Cache plus 8 MB L2 cache
  • 3rd generation Matrix Acceleration AI accelerators with ML-based upscaling
  • 16 GB of GDDR6 memory on a 256-bit bus at up to 20 Gbps
  • PCIe 5.0 support, with DisplayPort 2.1a and HDMI 2.1b outputs
